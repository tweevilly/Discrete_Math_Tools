Discrete Math Tools

Four small calculators built from the Excel sheets I made for my WGU discrete mathematics coursework. Each one shows its work and has short notes underneath.

  • MatricesMultiply two matrices, or find the determinant and inverse of a square matrix.
  • Sequences and seriesGeometric and arithmetic sequences: the nth term, a table of terms, sums, and convergence.
  • Truth tablesBuild a truth table for a logic expression and check whether two expressions are equivalent.
  • Row reductionSolve a system of linear equations and see every row operation.

About these tools

Answers use exact fractions, so 1/3 stays 1/3 instead of becoming 0.333. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

These are study aids from my own coursework (WGU discrete mathematics), not a replacement for working problems by hand.
